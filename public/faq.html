FAQ

shipping & delivery

Local deliveries within Ghana typically arrive within 7–10 business days of dispatch. International orders can take up to a month, depending on customs and the logistics provider. Tracking details are shared upon dispatch where available.

payment methods

We accept mobile money (MoMo) and card payments, processed securely through Paystack. All prices are listed in GHC. We never store your card or mobile money details.

sizing

Every product page has a size guide with measurements — open it before choosing a size. We do not offer size exchanges, so check carefully before you buy.

returns & exchanges

All confirmed sales are final — we don't accept returns or exchanges for sizing or change of mind. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, email studio@360sprng.com within 7 days of delivery with your order reference and photo evidence.

my order hasn't arrived

If your order is marked as delivered but you haven't received it, email studio@360sprng.com within 48 hours with your order reference.

still have a question?

Reach us on Instagram or in the community.
