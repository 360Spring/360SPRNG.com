a life was drawn for you before you could hold a pen.

deviation has a name. they call it waste.

we heard the word. we built regardless.

unsanctioned hours. borrowed tools. a vision held by few.

there are those who build. there are those who observe.

we build.

the frat is not addressed. it is recognized.

if this reached you, you already understood.

live beyond life.

beyond what was permitted. beyond what was expected. beyond the quiet erosion of becoming someone else's design.
