Community Guidelines

spaces covered: whatsapp · discord · instagram violations: immediate removal
← terms of use 01 — scope 02 — prohibited conduct 03 — consequences 04 — reporting 05 — moderation authority

01 — scope

These guidelines apply to all 360SPRNG community spaces — including our WhatsApp community, Discord server, and Instagram comment sections — and to all participants regardless of when they joined, what they have purchased, or their standing within the community.

Participation in any 360SPRNG space is a privilege, not a right. Violation of any guideline in this document is grounds for immediate removal. Access can be revoked at any time without notice or explanation.

02 — prohibited conduct

The following are not permitted in any 360SPRNG community space.

Do not harass anyone. This includes targeting individuals with repeated, unwanted, or hostile communication — including in direct messages initiated through our spaces.

Do not post hate speech. Content that attacks or dehumanises individuals or groups on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, nationality, or any other characteristic will result in immediate removal.

Do not spam. Unsolicited repetitive messages, bulk posting, or anything designed to disrupt the space is not welcome.

Do not self-promote. This is not a marketplace. Promoting your own brand, product, service, or platform without explicit permission from 360SPRNG is not permitted.

Do not impersonate. Do not represent yourself as 360SPRNG, a member of the team, or any other individual in a misleading way.

Do not reproduce or distribute 360SPRNG graphics, designs, or brand assets without explicit authorisation.

Do not spread misinformation about 360SPRNG, its products, drops, or team.

Do not share content that violates Ghanaian law or the laws of the jurisdiction in which you are located.

03 — consequences

Violation of any guideline results in immediate removal from the relevant 360SPRNG community space. There are no warnings. There is no appeals process.

Removal from one space does not automatically result in removal from others, but 360SPRNG reserves the right to remove an individual from all community spaces simultaneously where the violation warrants it.

Removal from a 360SPRNG community space does not constitute a ban from third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Discord, or Instagram, which are governed by their own terms of service.

Where we believe a violation constitutes a breach of applicable law, we reserve the right to report the conduct to the relevant platform or authorities.

04 — reporting

If you witness a violation of these guidelines in any 360SPRNG community space, report it via email at studio@360sprng.com.

Include the relevant community space, a description of the incident, and any supporting evidence where possible. All reports are reviewed by 360SPRNG directly.

05 — moderation authority

360SPRNG moderates all community spaces at its sole discretion. Moderation decisions are final.

These guidelines may be updated at any time. Continued participation in any 360SPRNG community space following an update constitutes acceptance of the revised guidelines. Current guidelines are always available at 360sprng.com/community-guidelines.
